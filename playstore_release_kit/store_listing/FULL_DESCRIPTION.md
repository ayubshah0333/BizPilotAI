# BizPilot AI — Autonomous Smart Business Operating System

Take full command of your retail shop, wholesale business, or trading store with **BizPilot AI** — the next-generation, offline-first Point of Sale (POS), Inventory Manager, Customer Udhaar Ledger, and AI Business Diagnostics platform.

Designed specifically for small and medium enterprises (SMEs), retailers, superstores, grocery shops, electronics outlets, and distributors. Works 100% offline without internet, and syncs seamlessly with your central store management database!

---

## 🚀 Key Features

### 🛒 1. Lightning-Fast POS & Smart Billing
- Rapid product search by Name, SKU, or Barcode.
- **Built-in Camera Barcode Scanner**: Scan barcodes and QR codes directly using your phone camera with no expensive hardware required!
- Real-time cart calculations, instant discounts, and split billing (Cash or Udhaar/Credit).
- Multi-currency support (PKR, USD, AED, SAR, INR, EUR).

### 🧾 2. Bluetooth Thermal Receipt Printing (58mm / 80mm)
- 1-tap wireless ESC/POS thermal printing to any Bluetooth, Wi-Fi, or USB receipt printer.
- Generates clean, professional receipts complete with store header, line item quantities, discounts, and invoice QR codes.
- Compatible with all standard 58mm (2-inch) and 80mm (3-inch) mini POS printers.

### 📲 3. WhatsApp Commerce & Instant Digital Receipts
- Send professional, formatted invoice receipts directly to customer WhatsApp with 1 tap.
- Send polite, cordial bilingual (English & Urdu) payment reminders to customers with outstanding Udhaar.
- Boost debt recovery by 40% with zero awkward conversations!

### 📒 4. Customer Udhaar / Khata Ledger
- Complete double-entry customer credit and payment ledger.
- Set individual customer credit limits to prevent bad debts.
- Chronological transaction history: track every credit sale and cash settlement with dynamic running balance calculation.

### 📦 5. Real-Time Inventory & Stock Management
- Real-time stock decrement upon every sale checkout.
- High-visibility stock badges: `In Stock`, `Low Stock Alert`, and `Out of Stock`.
- Custom low-stock threshold triggers so you never run out of bestsellers.
- Track Cost Price, Selling Price, and Gross Profit Margins per item.

### 🧠 6. AI Business Insights Engine
- **Top Grossing Drivers**: Identifies products that generate your highest profit margins.
- **Dead Stock Detector**: Detects slow-moving products and calculates your locked working capital.
- **Stockout Velocity**: Predicts exact days left before your high-demand items run out.
- **Bilingual Recommendations**: Practical, actionable advice in both English and professional Urdu.

### 🛡️ 7. 100% Offline-First Architecture
- Built on local high-performance SQLite engine — your shop never stops running even during internet or power outages.
- Automatic cloud synchronization whenever your connection is restored.
