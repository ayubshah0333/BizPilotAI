Privacy Policy for BizPilot AI

Last Updated: November 10, 2026

BizPilot AI ("we", "our", or "app") is developed to empower small and medium businesses with autonomous inventory, billing, POS, and customer credit ledger management. We prioritize user privacy and data security above all else.

1. Information We Collect

BizPilot AI is designed with an offline-first architecture. Your business information, products, customer names, phone numbers, and financial transactions are stored locally on your device in a secure SQLite database.

  • Camera Permission: Used exclusively in real-time to scan product barcodes and QR codes. No photos or video frames are captured, recorded, or transmitted to any external server.
  • Bluetooth Permission: Used solely to discover and connect to physical 58mm/80mm ESC/POS thermal receipt printers for printing customer receipts.
  • Customer Contact Info: Customer names and phone numbers entered into your Khata ledger remain under your sole control and are only used when you initiate a WhatsApp invoice or payment reminder.

2. Data Usage and Third-Party Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or monetize your personal or commercial data. All business sales numbers, inventory levels, and debt records belong strictly to you.

3. Data Security & Storage

Local data is stored securely in the app's sandboxed storage. If configured with a custom backend server, communications are secured through industry-standard HTTPS protocols.

4. Contact Information

For any privacy inquiries or support regarding BizPilot AI, contact us at: support@bizpilot.ai
